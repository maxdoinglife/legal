Privacy notice

Application: Max net worth · Operator: Max Lamprell (an individual, Sydney, Australia) · Last updated 25 October 2026

This is a personal, non-commercial application. It exists so that one person, Max Lamprell, can see the balances of his own bank accounts in one place. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its operator.

What data is processed

Account information only: account names and identifiers, balances, and the currency of each account. The data is read from accounts that Max Lamprell holds in his own name, with his explicit consent given at the bank through open banking. No payment is ever initiated. No data about any other person is processed.

How it is obtained

Through the account information service of Enable Banking, a regulated open banking provider, which acts as the technical intermediary between the bank and this application under its own terms and privacy policy. Each consent is granted at the bank, lasts at most the period the bank allows (90 days in the United Kingdom), and can be revoked at any time at the bank or at Enable Banking.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Balances are stored as daily snapshots on devices controlled by the operator. The data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose. Live market prices and exchange rates are fetched from public data feeds; no account data is sent to those feeds.

Retention

Balance snapshots are kept as personal financial records for as long as the operator finds them useful. Access credentials expire with the bank consent and are deleted when revoked.

Your rights

The only data subject is the operator himself. Should anyone else believe their data has been processed by this application, they may contact the data protection contact below and the matter will be dealt with promptly, including deletion.
